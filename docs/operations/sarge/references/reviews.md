# Reviews (background, every run)

Part of the [/sarge routine](../SKILL.md). Paths such as `scripts/` and `references/` are relative to the skill's directory.

- **Sweep:** PRs merged since the last check (default lookback 24h) in Sergeant's repos (terros-inc/sergeant, plus Sergeant-authored PRs in terros-inc/sales and terros-inc/terros-wiki), plus open PRs **parked on a human** (repos where humans merge, or a pending Sergeant question). Skip open PRs Sergeant is still actively working on: it merges within minutes, so those reviews go stale. A PR needs review when the ledger has no review of its final head. When unsure, include it. **terros-wiki PRs get low effort**: a one-line glance for anything clearly broken or wrongly removed, no link sweeps, never a third opinion (the wiki may be deleted and restarted one day).
- **Kick off** one read-only review scout per run for all of them, in the background, through the assistant's normal task spawning (never subagents that would count against the operator's quotas). Put the substance of `references/review-rubric.md` in the brief.
- **Reviewer AI ≠ author AI:** review with a different provider from the one Sergeant's workers use. Sergeant on Claude means review with another provider; Sergeant on another provider means review with Claude. Check the installation config's `runners` setting.
- **Sergeant's own review, per PR:** `scripts/snapshot.sh --reviews TECH-a,TECH-b` prints Sergeant's review telemetry. `trigger` is `required` (mandatory) or the random audit sample; it also shows the reviewer vs implementer vendor, the verdict, blocking/non-blocking counts, and the reviewed vs merged head. Record it in the ledger next to our verdict.
- **Flag PRs worth another opinion** (a 2nd if Sergeant didn't review it, a 3rd if it did): anything touching the merge gate, the task lifecycle (start/stop/resume, slots, budgets), credentials or trust boundaries, or Sergeant's question/answer loop. Tell the operator which ones in the batch, so they can run an additional review on just those. Everything else gets our one review.
- **Track who catches what** in the notes (Sergeant's reviewer, our reviewer, additional reviews), so extra opinions can be dropped once a trend shows they add nothing.
- **Exceptions to surface** (and keep counting in the notes): a **mandatory** Sergeant review that found nothing blocking while we found a correctness issue (note whether Sergeant flagged it as non-blocking or missed it); a **random-audit** PR where anyone found something; a PR **merged at a head Sergeant never reviewed**.
- **Don't wait for it.** When a review comes back, record each PR and its reviewed head SHA in the ledger. PRs with no issue get one summary line. PRs with findings become decision items: for an open PR, a copy-ready steer to post on its Linear issue once the operator agrees; for a merged PR, a proposed follow-up ticket (title, scope, rubric priority), with permission asked once for the set.
